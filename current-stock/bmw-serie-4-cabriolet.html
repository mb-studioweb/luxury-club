    Location de voitures de luxe > Nos véhicules > Bmw > BMW Série 4 Cabriolet

    Based on a G-series 911 coupe 1985 it benefits from the Bosch electronic fuel injection and updated 915 5-speed transmission. Clubsport brakes and Bilstein sport suspension. Beautifully executed to pass as an original classic 911 from the 70s with leather and houndstooth interior. No frill just a classic drivers car on the outside with the more modern underpinnings and stiffer chassis of its later brother.

    The car has had very minimal use in the past few years and presents in stunning condition. It will undergo a full service and check over through the Luxury Club workshop for complete peace of mind

    Tax, MOT and ULEZ exempt being first registered over 40 years ago.
