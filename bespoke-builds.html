01

Initial

meeting

Begin the conversation with us in our showroom in the Cotswolds.

In order to gain some insights into our process, our team will introduce you to some of our inspiring current projects and show you around our workshop.

The initial meeting also includes learning more about your driving history and preferences to best create a build that caters to you specifically. Whether you’d like a Restomod for day-to-day drives, European touring, or racing, we can personalise your drive to suit your needs.

Whether you’re starting from scratch or have a suitable donor car and a specific vision already (or somewhere in the middle), let us help you map out the process.

02

Choose

model

If you don’t have a suitable donor car already, our in-house team of specialists will guide you through the decision making process, suggesting which model may suit your vision the best,  and source whichever model you prefer. Your donor-car will then be brought to our workshop, ready for stage 3, choosing your Rennsport model.

All models are available in both Coupe and Targa versions, as well as RHD & LHD.

03

Conceptualise

At this stage, we delve into specifics.

Let’s talk power, brakes, parts, finishes, electronic suspension, AC, colour, or anything else you’d like to. It’s your vision, and it’s our job to actualise it. Whether you’d like your car to match the colour of your favourite item of clothing, or you’d like panels covered in genuine Shagreen Stingray skin, (both of which are real examples we’ve encountered at Rennsport), this is the stage where we discuss your dreams and ideas.

The Conceptualise stage ensures that your Rennsport 911 is not just a car, but a signature.

Technicality aside, we also pride ourselves on creating some of the most stylistically exciting and luxurious 911s, too. Whatever you would like your interiors to look like, we can cater to it - whether you’d like a specific set of instruments, steering wheel, or dashboard, we have a wide range of premium materials and options available.

At Rennsport, we only hire the best Porsche specialised technicians. Our team will be able to advise you on which engine and transmission will best serve your car requirements, and provide other technical recommendations.

Building my car with Rennsport was the realisation of a dream and Paul and the team went out of their way at every stage to ensure that the build experience is as much part of the dream as owning and driving the finished product.
- Private customer
Project no. 124
Goodwood green 911 ST 3.4
1987 911 Carrera 3.2 Coupe G50

04

Spec

After careful analysis of the requests made in the conceptualisation phase,  our team will next provide you with a broken-down specification and quote. We offer support in advising which special parts should be prioritised, and what may or may not be possible to ensure the most rewarding drive.

At this stage we will also provide an indication of build timeline and when you can expect to collect your Rennsport 911.

Here at Rennsport, we’ll closely guide you through the specification that you want. Our knowledge and experience mean that we can offer a comprehensive breakdown of which parts are, or aren’t, necessary for your drive.

"The quality does not end with the car. Paul and his team at 911Rennsport are passionate about the work and service they provide. Nothing is too much for them and I especially appreciate the way they tell me that I have a hair-brained upgrade that would be a complete waste of time and money. They deliver much more than “what is on the tin” and are the go-to Porsche specialists."
- David Hunter

05

Build

Visit our workshop to see your vision come to life firsthand. At this stage, your bespoke Porsche 911 Restomod is crafted from the ground up by our  technicians. Here at Rennsport, you are the designer, so we know how important it is that you’re involved in the process. Across several months, we carefully construct your dream car, offering multiple opportunities for you to come and witness the progress.

Learn more

The entire process from start to finish typically takes 12 months, so if there are adjustments you’d like to make in the meantime to specific parts, let us know and we can make it happen.
